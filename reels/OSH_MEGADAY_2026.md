# OSH Mega Day 2026 — "Mental Health Can Be Fun" (30 s aftermovie)

Vertical 9:16 highlight of Dr Fakrul Azren's mental health session at OSH Mega Day 2026, cut from the
[Google Photos album](https://photos.app.goo.gl/KgPWsuoEcFJXqtQJA): 24 videos (5.1 min) and 3 photos.

**Deliverable:** `release/OSH_MegaDay_2026_Highlight_9x16.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, exactly 30.000 s,
H.264 High + AAC 48 kHz, −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP. This works for Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels and
YouTube Shorts. Text stays inside the social safe zone: x 90–990 px, y 270–1450 px.

Everything you see is real event footage. Faces, clothing and venues come straight from the clips. Only
exposure, colour and stabilisation are adjusted. No people, activities or reactions were generated.
Higgsfield made just two abstract light-leak plates with no people in them.

## Picture

- **Plates** (`tools/osh_plates.py`): each shot is trimmed, then:
  - stabilised with two-pass vidstab and auto zoom
  - retimed (1.3× speed-ups, 0.6× and 0.8× frame-blended slow motion, no optical-flow warping on faces)
  - exposure-matched with a per-shot gamma toward a common mean luma
  - lightly denoised
  - graded one way for all shots: contrast 1.06, vibrance +0.22 (protects skin), warm highlights,
    slightly cool shadows, light CAS sharpening
- **Freeze-frame sticker** (16.4–17.0 s): camera flash and shutter, then:
  - blue multiply tint and rotating yellow rays behind
  - a subject cut-out (`hyperframes remove-background` plus a manual garbage matte to drop people in
    the background) pops forward with a white sticker outline
  - an "OSH MEGA DAY 2026" paper tag
- **Light leaks** (Higgsfield, screen-blended): warm at 0–1.7 s and 16.8–19.5 s, blue at 20.8–22.5 s and
  26.6–30 s.

## Motion graphics (HyperFrames)

The composition is `reel/index.html`, generated by `tools/osh_compose.py` from `reels/osh_megaday_2026.json`.
It is one paused GSAP timeline, so every frame can be seeked and renders the same way each time.

- **Kinetic type:** Poppins Black/ExtraBold with navy outline and hard colour shadows. The words come in
  letter by letter with overshoot, on torn paper-cut strips.
- **Doodles:** hand-drawn SVG squiggles, burst lines, an arrow pointing at the speaker and a sprout. Each
  draws on with stroke reveals; sparkles twinkle on the beat.
- **Hearts:** the 💙 is drawn as a blue SVG heart with a white outline, so it renders the same on every
  platform.
- **Closing type:**
  - "EVERY CONVERSATION" in Poppins SemiBold (tracked) with a mask reveal
  - "MATTERS." in Playfair Display Black Italic, letters blur in
  - "Spread Positivity" in Caveat with a wipe reveal
  - credit pill: "Dr Fakrul Azren | Mental Health Speaker"
- **Camera and transitions:**
  - camera moves: crash zoom, settle, drift, push, punch-in, beat-synced shake, Ken Burns on the still
  - transitions: whip pans (motion blur and overscale), a zoom-through, white flashes on impacts
  - finish: vignette and fade to black
- **Palette:** blue #2B6BFF / navy #0F2A6B, white, yellow #FFD23F, soft orange #FF9F5A.

    The music ducks ~3 dB under these.

  - The final mix is loudness-normalised (two-pass EBU R128) to −14 LUFS integrated.

## Higgsfield

The light leaks are the only generated material. Both were made with Kling 3.0 Turbo, text-to-video,
9:16, 1080p, 6 s, at 12 credits each.

1. **Warm:** "Abstract cinematic film light leak plate on a pure black background: soft warm golden-orange
   and pale yellow light flares and organic bokeh glows drifting slowly across the frame, gentle anamorphic
   streaks, 16mm film burn feel, smooth slow motion. No people, no objects, no text, only light on black."
2. **Blue:** "Abstract cinematic light leak plate on a pure black background: soft sky-blue, white and light
   cyan lens flares and dreamy bokeh orbs sweeping gently from the left edge to the right, prismatic
   glints, airy and uplifting, smooth slow motion. No people, no objects, no text, only light on black."

Higgsfield's audio tools only make speech, so the music is synthesized locally instead.

Some image-to-video and "cinematic camera" models re-render the people in the frame, so they were not used
on event footage. The push-ins, speed ramps, shake, motion blur and leaks are all applied deterministically
to the real frames. That keeps everyone looking exactly as they did.

## Rebuild

```bash
# album -> work/src/cNN.mp4 / .jpg (see the session notes; Google Photos "=dv" originals)
python3 tools/osh_plates.py reels/osh_megaday_2026.json work/src reel/assets/plates
python3 tools/osh_music.py reel/assets/audio/bgm.wav
python3 tools/osh_sfx.py reel/assets/audio/sfx
python3 tools/osh_mix.py reels/osh_megaday_2026.json work/src reel/assets/audio
python3 tools/osh_compose.py reels/osh_megaday_2026.json reel/index.html
cd reel && npx hyperframes@0.8.181 lint && npx hyperframes@0.8.181 render . -q delivery --fps 30 -o renders/master.mp4
```

Retiming a shot, swapping a clip or changing a title only needs an edit to `reels/osh_megaday_2026.json`.
Then rerun plates (for that shot: `osh_plates.py … s09`), mix and compose.

To use a licensed track instead of the synthesized one, replace `reel/assets/audio/bgm.wav` (30 s, 120 BPM,
first downbeat at 1.0 s) and rerun the mix.
