## Instagram / Facebook (English)

**Menjunjung Kasih. Forum Intelektual Diraja 2026** 🤍

Alhamdulillah. It was a profound honour to serve as a panellist at the Forum Intelektual Diraja 2026, held in the presence of Her Majesty Raja Permaisuri Agong, Raja Zarith Sofiah, Chancellor of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia.

Under the theme **"Adab and Intellect in the Digital World: Building a Society of Wisdom"**, we spoke about the mental health of young people growing up with screens and social media, and how to raise people who use technology without losing control of themselves.

> "And it is this space that truly shapes our intellect, manners, wisdom and responsibility as human beings."

My sincere thanks to the organisers, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, my fellow panellists and the moderator, and to everyone who attended and asked such meaningful questions.

📍 Dewan Sultan Iskandar, UTM Johor Bahru · 🗓 9 September 2026
